FIELD NOTES

Cloud Support Engineer checklist

When CPU alarms fire, investigate before assuming the instance is broken.

  • Confirm the instance ID, metric, statistic, period, and threshold.
  • Check the alarm history and whether enough datapoints were evaluated.
  • Review application logs, process activity, and recent traffic changes.
  • Verify the alarm action, SNS topic, email subscription confirmation, and delivery status.
  • Remember: notification is not remediation. Scaling or automated recovery needs additional configuration.
